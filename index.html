02 The delicious backstory

Good cookies.
No secret handshakes.

Crumb & Co. began with a borrowed mixer, a stubborn belief that “one more chunk” is always the right answer, and the kind of kitchen mess worth cleaning up.

Today, we make every batch with proper butter, thoughtful chocolate, and enough time for the dough to become its best self. No shortcuts hiding in the fine print—just generous cookies baked for the people you like best.

  • 01

    Rested overnight

    Time deepens the flavor and gives each cookie its signature chewy middle.

  • 02

    Honest ingredients

    Real butter, fragrant vanilla, thoughtful chocolate. Nothing you need a decoder for.

  • 03

    Baked in small runs

    We favor attention over volume—and crisp edges over conveyor belts.
